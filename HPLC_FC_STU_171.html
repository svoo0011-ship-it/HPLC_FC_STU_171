Min %B — log K vs %B this ligand's logged runs

Reproduces the course's own "RETENTION CYCLE" worksheet calculation: fit log K against %B using this ligand's logged runs, then solve that line for the %B whose predicted tR lands exactly on the run-time spec. The highest %B tested is always left out of the fit — at that %B the peaks are least separated, so its tR is closer to an estimate than a measurement. This is exactly what feeds the "Intermediate %B" / "calculated Min %B floor" decisions above; nothing here is specific to any one compound or ligand.
